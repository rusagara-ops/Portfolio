About

A bit about me

I’m a computer science major at Yale (Class of 2027) who enjoys building full-stack products, from clean frontends to solid backends and the glue that connects them. I like taking messy, real-world problems and turning them into software that’s simple to operate and straightforward to extend.

Recently, that’s looked like shipping MCP-based automation and AI agents at Dispatch Energy, building hyperspectral imaging infrastructure at Yale–NSF, and designing a HIPAA-aligned home-care platform used in a live clinical pilot. I care about clear abstractions, good docs, and collaborating closely with the people who use what I build.
