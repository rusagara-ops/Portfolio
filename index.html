About

A bit about me

I’m a computer science major at Yale (Class of 2027) who enjoys building full-stack products, from clean frontends to solid backends and the glue that connects them. I like taking messy, real-world problems and turning them into software that’s simple to operate and straightforward to extend.

Recently, that’s looked like building infrastructure for a hyperspectral imaging ecosystem at Yale–NSF, automating RF testing workflows at UL Solutions, and shipping a bug-tracking backend that my team actually depended on day to day. I care about clear abstractions, good docs, and collaborating closely with the people who use what I build.
